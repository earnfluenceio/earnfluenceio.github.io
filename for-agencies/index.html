The agency problem

Affiliate software wasn't built for agencies.

Most platforms assume one merchant, one program. That's not how an agency works.

— Pricing punishes growth

Per-affiliate and per-program fees stack across a client roster.

— Payouts run client by client

Most tools hand you a CSV and tell you to figure out the rest.
