The creator problem

Affiliate networks weren't built for creators.

Most platforms treat creators as line items. Old dashboards, opaque tracking, payouts when they feel like it.

— Dashboards from 2014

Clunky interfaces, delayed data, no mobile view. Not built for how creators actually work.

— Opaque attribution

You're told what you earned. You're not shown why. Refund clawbacks come without warning.

— Payouts when they get to it

Net-60, minimum thresholds, manual processing. Your money sits on someone else's balance sheet.

— Programs that don't fit you

Random brands you've never heard of, expecting you to promote them to your audience anyway.

Common questions

Things creators ask first.

Is Earnfluence free for creators?

Yes. Creators never pay. Brands and agencies pay for the platform.

How do I find programs to join?

Brands and agencies invite creators directly. Sign up free and you'll be on their radar for future programs in your niche.

How often do I get paid?

Each program sets its own schedule, typically monthly. Payouts run automatically once approved.

What happens with refunds?

If a sale you drove gets refunded, the commission is clawed back. You'll see it in your dashboard the moment it happens. No surprise deductions.

Is there a minimum payout?

Each program sets its own threshold. Many have none.

Can I be in multiple programs?

Yes. Every program you join shows up in one dashboard. Switch between them or see aggregate earnings.
