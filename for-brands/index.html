Common questions

Things brands ask first.

How long does setup actually take?

Under ten minutes for most brands. Connect Stripe, PayPal, or WooCommerce, set your commission rules, generate your first invite links.

What if I already have creators I work with?

Bring them in. Send single invites or bulk-invite a list via CSV. Creators get a dashboard the same day.

What commission types do you support?

Flat, percentage, or recurring. Recurring commissions can be lifetime or time-bounded (e.g., 12 months), configurable per program.

What about subscriptions and refunds?

Recurring commissions, refunds, and cancellations are tracked automatically. If a customer cancels and resubscribes within 90 days, the original creator picks up commission again.

Can I work with an agency on Earnfluence later?

Yes. Many of our agency partners can take over running your program. If you'd like a more seamless handoff with full data continuity, that's on the roadmap.

How does pricing work?

Flat pricing, no transaction fees. We're finalizing tiers now. Request access and we'll share details directly.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Start free with no credit card. Upgrade when your program is generating real revenue.
